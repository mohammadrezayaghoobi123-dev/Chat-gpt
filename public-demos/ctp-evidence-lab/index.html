Evidence Lab

A transparent, local-only demo for reviewing a claim against a source. It records what the source says, what it does not establish, and whether the submitted evidence supports or contradicts the claim.

Prototype boundary: This demo does not browse the web, authenticate sources, or prove truth automatically. Results are based only on the text you enter. “Supported” means the entered source text appears to support the claim—not that the claim is independently verified.
